A note from me

Hi, I'm Vish. Welcome in.

I help brands work with creators, and I make sure the work actually runs. For five years I did this at influencer agencies: finding the right creators, writing the briefs, agreeing fees and usage, keeping approvals on time and reporting what happened. Along the way I worked with more than 850 creators for 20+ brands, from Philips and L’Oréal to Universal Pictures and Bumble.

I came to it through media school and a small trekking company I ran on the side. By 2024 I was leading client servicing for brand accounts and a team of twelve, fully remote. The job taught me that most campaign problems start early, with a vague brief or the wrong creator, so that is where I put the most care.

Today I'm based in Germany, and I build AI tools for the parts of the job that eat the most time: shortlists, contracts and reports. I've built 11 of them, all open source, without being an engineer, and more than 9,000 people follow what I share about it on LinkedIn.

Six things I help with

  1. Influencer campaignsThe right creators, clear briefs, fair fees, approvals on time and an honest report.
  2. Talent managementPitching creators to brands, then handling fees, usage and deliverables.
  3. Account managementOne senior point of contact for your brand clients, so nothing slips.
  4. Content and UGCShort videos scripted, filmed and edited, with captions in your voice.
  5. Team and operationsHiring, training and one shared system for tasks, files and deadlines.
  6. AI workflowsHuman-checked tools for scoring creators, checking contracts and reporting.

Questions

What people ask first.

How does working together start?

With a short intro call. We agree the goal and the scope, then you get a written proposal with timeline, price and what's not included.

Do you work remotely?

Yes, fully remote from Germany, on European time. I've led remote teams and run campaigns across markets.

Do you need access to my accounts?

Only when the task needs it, such as community management or ad reporting. Shortlists, briefs, videos and reports can be delivered as files.

Who do you usually work with?

Brands and D2C teams, agencies that need senior support, and founders building their own content.

How is the price set?

By scope, not by the hour. Each package shows a starting price; bigger or mixed projects get a custom quote.

Do I have to commit for months?

No. Start with a one-time pilot. Monthly packages can be paused or cancelled with notice.
