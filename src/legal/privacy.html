Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026. This page covers the Build with Vish website (buildwithvish.netlify.app), its newsletter and the automatic Instagram replies run by Vish Kumbhar for his Instagram account.

Automatic Instagram replies

When you comment a keyword (for example "CITED") on one of our Instagram posts or Reels, Instagram sends us that comment: its text, its ID, and your Instagram user ID and username. We use them only to send you one private message with the free guide you asked for, and a short public reply under your comment. We don't store your comment or your profile after the reply is sent, we don't add you to any list, and we never message you again unless you write to us.

The replies use Instagram's official API. Meta's own data policy applies to everything on Instagram.

Newsletter

If you sign up, your email address goes to our email provider (MailerLite), which asks you to confirm before anything is sent. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

Website

The site sets no tracking cookies and loads no third-party scripts.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, at any time: see data deletion. Contact: a direct message to Vish on Instagram or LinkedIn.
