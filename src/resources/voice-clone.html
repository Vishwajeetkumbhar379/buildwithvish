1. Why I did this

I was always insecure about my voice. On camera I speak slowly, and I hear every pause. So I cloned it. My first clone sounded flat and far away, like I was reading from across a room. The fixes below are what changed that. The clip in my reel is my clone telling a story, partly in French. I don't speak French.

2. Record the sample (this decides 80% of the quality)

DoWhy
1 to 2 minutes of clean speech for an Instant Voice CloneElevenLabs' own advice. More than 3 minutes adds little and can make it worse.
A quiet, soft room (a wardrobe full of clothes works)The clone copies your room. Echo in the sample means echo in every line it ever says. That was my mistake.
Mic or phone a hand's width from your mouthClose sounds warm. Far sounds like a recording of a recording.
One voice, steady volume, your natural energySpeak the way you'd tell a friend a story, not the way you'd read a script.
No music, no second person, no long silencesAnything that isn't you gets cloned too.

Want it even closer? A Professional Voice Clone trains on 30 minutes to 3 hours of audio and takes longer, but it's the most accurate option.

3. The settings that made it sound like me

  1. Use the newest model. I use Eleven v4. It handles emotion far better than the older models I tried.
  2. Write for the ear. Short sentences. Questions. A word in capitals for stress. Dialogue. The model performs the punctuation you give it.
  3. Add emotion cues in brackets, like [laughs], [serious] or [whispering], before the line. In my tests v4 acts them out and doesn't read them aloud. Use one or two per paragraph, not every line.
  4. Clean it once. Run the result through ElevenLabs' Voice Isolator to remove any room left in the voice.
  5. Speed it up a little. 1.1x to 1.2x with the pitch kept sounds energetic, not chipmunky.
  6. Keep the breaths. Never cut the pauses to zero. A short breath between sentences is what makes it sound human.

4. The prompts I give Claude

  1. Script for the ear: Rewrite this script so it sounds spoken, not written: short sentences, one question, one moment of stress in capitals, and at most two emotion cues in brackets. Keep my words where you can. [PASTE SCRIPT]
  2. Generate: Generate this with my ElevenLabs voice [VOICE ID] on the newest model, using my API key from the environment. Save voice_raw.mp3.
  3. Polish: Clean voice_raw.mp3 with the ElevenLabs Voice Isolator, speed it to [1.15]x with ffmpeg atempo so the pitch stays the same, shorten any pause longer than 0.4 s to 0.4 s with soft crossfades, and level it to -16 LUFS. Then transcribe it and show me any word that doesn't match the script.

5. Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
It sounds far away or echoeyRe-record the sample in a softer room, closer to the mic. Isolating the output helps, but the sample is the real fix.
It sounds flatNewest model, shorter sentences, one emotion cue per paragraph.
It's too slowSpeed up 1.1x to 1.2x with the pitch kept.
Sentences end abruptlyKeep a short breath between sentences and fade each line out, never a hard cut to silence.
It reads a cue out loudMove the cue onto its own line before the sentence, or remove it.
